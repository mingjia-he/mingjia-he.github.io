### Main results
- **Co-investment with payoff sharing** lets self-interested regional operators jointly invest in shared infrastructure and split the gains, benefiting both operators and users (Sioux Falls and Zürich–Winterthur case studies).
- **An iterative VCG-based mechanism** coordinates cross-border railway investment across European regions and improves overall network performance. 🏆 IEEE CSS TC on Smart Cities Outstanding Student Paper Prize 2026.
- **Model-free feedback control** steers a tri-level game between a municipality, competing operators, and travelers. On the Zürich multimodal network, only policies that subsidize transfers between public transport and taxis benefit both operators.

### Project description
In this project, we will investigate the idea of strategic interactions of future mobility stakeholders across heterogeneous regions, such as urban areas, agglomerations, and rural areas, leveraging techniques from network design, optimization, game theory, and policy making. 


### Tasks

1. Provide a mathematical formulation of the problem, describing the interaction patterns between different stakeholders (mobility providers, municipalities, travelers, policy makers, investors, etc.), and characterizing their interests and constraints; 
2. Define solution concepts, leveraging (various) notions of system equilibria;
3. Provide theoretical and algorithmic tools to solve the presented problems. 
implement by extending existing simulation tools, and real world case studies will be targeted to future Swiss scenarios.
